Why we started.

Every few months another leak makes the news, and every time it's someone's private information that ends up out in the open. We kept coming back to the same thought: if your data leaves your building, you are trusting someone else to guard it.

We think the safer answer is to keep it home. So we go to the people who hold sensitive information, learn how they actually work, and build the hardware and software around them. It stays on their premises, under their control.

We want every organization to own its intelligence layer, and to use AI with confidence, control and measurable impact.
